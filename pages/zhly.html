热烈祝贺张伦卓同学2025年5月22日完成本科毕业论文答辩

升华社

升华社2025年5月22日电(记者：呜呜) 伴随着五月的暖风和莘莘学子的青春步伐，张伦卓同学于近日圆满完成了其本科阶段的毕业论文，为四年求学画上了圆满的句号。这一重要成果不仅凝聚了张伦卓同学的勤奋努力，也体现了指导教师的悉心指导和学校优良的学术氛围。

据悉，张同学的毕业论文题目为《小型高频开关电源设计》，围绕专业核心问题展开深入研究，内容系统、结构严谨，体现了扎实的专业理论基础和较强的实践能力。在论文撰写过程中，他坚持理论联系实际，善于发现问题、分析问题，并提出了富有创新性的见解和解决方案，展现出良好的科研素养和学术潜力。

指导教师胡教授评价道：“张同学勤奋刻苦，严谨细致，具备良好的学术态度和独立思考能力。在毕业论文的选题、资料收集、实验设计及论文写作等环节均表现出极高的责任感和专业素养，论文质量达到了较高水平。”

学校相关负责人表示，张同学的顺利毕业，充分展现了学校坚持立德树人根本任务、培养高素质人才的办学理念和成效。学校将一如既往地为广大学子提供优质的教学资源和良好的学术环境，助力学生成长成才。

四年的大学时光，是求知的历程，也是成长的见证。张同学以实际行动诠释了新时代大学生的精神风貌，未来必将在学术道路和职业生涯中继续发光发热。全体师生衷心祝愿张同学前程似锦，乘风破浪，再创佳绩。

BBC

Uplift Student Dormitory, 22 May 2025 (Reporter: Wuwu)

In the gentle warmth of late spring, student Zhang Lunzhuo has successfully completed his undergraduate thesis, marking a significant milestone after four years of study. This achievement reflects not only Zhang’s diligence and perseverance but also the dedicated guidance from his supervisors and the university’s strong academic environment.

Zhang’s thesis, titled “Design of a Compact High-Frequency Switching Power Supply,” addresses key technical challenges in his field with in-depth research. The paper is structured rigorously and contains comprehensive content, demonstrating a solid theoretical foundation alongside practical expertise. Throughout the process, Zhang combined theory with practice effectively, showing a keen ability to identify and analyse problems and propose innovative solutions, highlighting his strong research potential and academic competence.

Professor Hu, Zhang’s supervisor, praised his work, stating, “Zhang has shown great dedication, meticulousness, and an excellent academic attitude. From topic selection to data collection, experimental design, and writing, he exhibited high responsibility and professionalism, which ensured the high quality of his thesis.”

University officials noted that Zhang’s successful graduation exemplifies the institution’s commitment to nurturing well-rounded talents through moral and intellectual education. The university remains dedicated to providing excellent educational resources and a supportive academic environment to help students thrive.

The four years of university life have been a journey of knowledge and personal growth. Zhang San’s achievements embody the spirit of the modern generation of students. Looking ahead, he is expected to continue excelling in both academic research and his professional career. Faculty and peers alike extend their best wishes for his bright future and continued success.
